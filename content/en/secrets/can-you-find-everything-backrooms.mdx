## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you find everything Backrooms titles offer in a single playthrough?
In static, story-focused games, yes—careful players who inspect every room and solve all puzzles can achieve 100% completion in one run. However, games that use randomized generation, branching exits, or percentage-based room spawns require multiple playthroughs across different map seeds to see every possible environment and collectible.

### How do you tell an intentional secret wall from an out-of-bounds bug?
Intentional secrets typically feature subtle developer hints: misaligned textures, a distinct humming sound, altered carpet coloration, or an interact prompt. Accidental map seams usually show empty grey voids, lack collision entirely, or cause the player camera to jitter unsteadily when approached.

### Do items respawn if you backtrack to previous levels?
In most titles, physical survival items (such as batteries and health restoration drinks) remain fixed once looted, though some dynamic systems replenish basic supplies after long intervals. Unique lore items, notes, and keycards never duplicate or respawn once collected.

### What is the best strategy if you get completely lost while secret hunting?
If your orientation fails, immediately stop wandering into new halls. Return to the last marked intersection or identifiable landmark (such as a pillar cluster or broken light fixture). Begin using the perimeter wall-following method from that anchor point rather than making random turns through central corridors.
