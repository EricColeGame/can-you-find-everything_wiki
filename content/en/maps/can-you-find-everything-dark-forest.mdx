## Step-by-Step Map Sweeping Strategy for 100% Clearance

Relying on random wandering in dense foliage leads to backtracking, disorienting loops, and wasted play sessions. To guarantee complete coverage, adopt a methodical surveying framework derived from real-world search-and-rescue protocols.

### 1. Phase 1: The Outer Rim Sweep
Always begin by tracing the outer boundary walls of the zone. Dark forest edges frequently conceal cliff-face caves, hidden mountain passes, and boundary-straddling campsites. Hug the impassable cliffs or zone borders until you complete a full 360-degree perimeter circuit, placing custom map markers at any impassable obstacles or locked gates.

### 2. Phase 2: Hydrological & Landmark Tracing
Before sweeping interior zones, follow all natural linear features. Streams, rivers, ridgelines, and worn animal trails naturally funnel players toward major ruins and fishing or gathering hotspots. Charting waterways first also splits wide zones into manageable quadrants.

### 3. Phase 3: Parallel Grid Transects
Once the perimeter and waterways are established, systematically clear interior quadrants using parallel sweeps. Move across the terrain in parallel lines spaced roughly 40 to 60 meters apart—or within standard line-of-sight distance under the canopy. Whenever you encounter thick brush, circle it completely before resuming your bearing.

### 4. Phase 4: Nocturnal Verification
A significant percentage of hidden woodland encounters activate only under specific environmental conditions. Return to major clearings, altars, and stone circles at night to verify whether dormant objects awaken or spectral NPCs appear.
