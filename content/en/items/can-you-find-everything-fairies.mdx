## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you find everything fairies without using the in-game hint system?
Yes, every stage is designed to be fully solvable through visual deduction alone. If you find yourself stuck, switching your scanning style to the 3x3 quadrant method or temporarily dimming ambient room lighting can make faint wing highlights stand out clearly without penalizing your hint score.

### What is the best way to handle colorblindness when searching for fairies?
Many modern search titles feature accessibility toggles that add high-contrast borders or color shifts to collectible items. If your game lacks dedicated colorblind modes, adjusting your monitor's digital vibrancy and sharpness settings can help distinguish muted green and red fairy sprites from natural background foliage.

### Why do some fairies refuse to register when I click on them?
If clicking a visible fairy fails to register, the item is likely linked to an active trigger sequence. You may need to click an obstruction—such as opening a lantern cage or moving an obstructing branch—before the hit box unlocks for collection.

### Do hidden fairies make audio sounds when you get close?
In many search-and-find titles, faint environmental audio cues play when your cursor lingers near a hidden sprite. You might hear gentle wind chimes, soft fluttering, or a subtle sparkle sound effect. Playing with headphones helps you triangulate elusive targets through spatial audio.
