## Critical Missables: Where Most Players Fail

The biggest obstacle to acquiring every single collectible stems from irreversible narrative milestones. Developers frequently trigger structural changes to environments—known as world-state progression—after major cutscenes or boss encounters. 

```
Prologue Zone ➔ Point of No Return ➔ Mid-Game Shift ➔ Post-Game Lockout
```

When evaluating whether **can you find everything all items** on your first attempt, watch out for the following common structural barriers:

1. **Burned or Destroyed Hubs**: Towns or sanctuaries that serve as early quest centers may be attacked or altered, permanently removing resident vendors and their exclusive inventories.
2. **Branching Faction Allegiances**: Choosing to align with one guild or syndicate often cuts ties with rivals, locking their unique armor sets and weapons behind alternative playthroughs.
3. **Timed Drop Windows**: Defeating a world boss or completing a puzzle within a specific time window or under specialized combat conditions.
4. **Dialogue-Dependent Rewards**: Selecting aggressive or dismissive dialogue choices can cause non-player characters to withhold specific trinkets or quest items.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you find everything all items in a single playthrough?
In many titles, yes, provided you research non-reversible narrative milestones and avoid mutually exclusive faction locks. However, games featuring diverging storylines or distinct ending-exclusive weapons may require either a targeted save reload or a New Game Plus cycle to obtain every theoretical asset.

### What should I do if a quest item glitches or disappears?
First, attempt a complete game restart and fast-travel out of the zone to force cell reloading. If the object remains missing, check your quest log to confirm an unfulfilled pre-requisite. Community reports often suggest checking local lost-and-found vendors or junk dealers, as some games redirect dropped unique items to specific shops.

### Do items stored in containers despawn over time?
World containers like enemy chests and outdoor crates usually reset during environment respawn cycles. To keep your collected inventory safe, store your gear strictly inside dedicated player homes, persistent camp trunks, or your character's immediate personal storage.

### Does difficulty setting affect whether you can discover all gear?
Generally, game difficulty alters enemy damage, health pools, and encounter intensity rather than item availability. That said, select games lock elite cosmetic skins or mastercraft weapon blueprints behind hardcore modes or specific boss challenge runs.
